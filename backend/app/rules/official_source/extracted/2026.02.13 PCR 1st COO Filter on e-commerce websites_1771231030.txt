MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Consumer Affairs) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th February, 2026 
 
G.S.R. 128(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (j) and (q) of  
sub-section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules,  2011, namely:- 
  
1. Short title and commencement. – (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) 
Amendment Rules, 2026. 
  
 
(2) They shall come into force on the 1st day of July, 2026. 
  
2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, in rule 6, after sub-rule (10), the following sub-rule 
shall be inserted, namely:- 
  
“(10A) Every e-commerce entity selling imported products shall provide the product listings of such imported products 
in a searchable and sortable filter specifying the country of origin.”. 
  
 [F. No. I-9/5/2023-W&M] 
 ANUPAM MISHRA, Addl. Secy.
