NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the4th December, 2014
G.S.R. 870(E).In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clause (G)
of sub-section (2) of Section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central
Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged
Commodities) Rules,2011,namely:
1. (1)These fules may be called as the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)(Second)
(Amendment)Rules,2014.
(2) They shall come into force from the date oftheir publication in Official Gazette.
2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 in rule 26, after clause (d),
thefollowingclause shallbeinserted,namely:
"(e) any threadwhich is sold in coil to handloom weavers.".
