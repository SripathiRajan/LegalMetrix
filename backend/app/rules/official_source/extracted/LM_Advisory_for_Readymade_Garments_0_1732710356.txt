Sir,
The undersigned is directed to refer to the references received from M/o Textiles and
2.In this context, in continuation of this Department's letter No. WM-10(5)/2016 dated
16th September, 2016 it is further advised that:
(0)The mandatorylabeling requirements underthe LegalMetrology(Packaged Commodities)
Rules,2011areapplicableonly forpre-packaged commodities,defined underSection2(0)of
the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
(ii)The mandatory labeling requirements for pre-packaged commodities are therefore not
applicable to garments sold in loose form.
(ili)Thelabeling requirements for garments sold in loose form in retail stores may include
only thefollowing declaration w.e.f.1st July2017:-
(a)Name/Descriptionoftheproduct
(b) Size: Internationally recognizable size indicators -S,M, L, XL, etc. along with details in
metric notation in terms of cm or m as case may be.
(c) MRP
(d)Name,Fulladdress&CustomerCareNo.ofthemanufacturer

3. In the case of readymade garments sold to consumer in Pre-packaged form,declarations
required underthe Rules may be made, Further, the size of the garments needs to be
mentioned in metres or centimetres, as the case may be, and any additional detail such as S,
M, L, XL, XXL etc.may be treated as additional declaration.
4.This issues withtheapproval oftheCompetentAuthority.

The undersigned is directed to refer to your letter No. P-6/776/17 dated 7th February,
2017 on the above mentioned subject and to state that:
(i)A loosegarments which is soldafter consumer sees them for Style/Design, tries them for
fit and touches them for feel of the Fabric/ Fiber etc., is not a pre-packaged commodity if the
same garment is delivered to the consumer
(ii) your members may comply with the guidelines at their choice but not later than
01.07.2017.
(ili) the words"TO FIT SIZE" instead of only"Size" on Garments sold in loose form, may be
usedasper convenience.
